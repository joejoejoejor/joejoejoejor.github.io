Valuing MBS Options

A comprehensive analysis of Mortgage-Backed Securities valuation incorporating prepayment options. This project implements the Nelson-Siegel yield curve model, constructs a Black-Derman-Toy short rate tree, and analyzes the impact of prepayment options on MBS value, duration, and convexity. Includes full MATLAB implementation with detailed mathematical derivations.
